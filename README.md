## 🌟 Highlights

- 🏡 **Interactive Landing Page** – with offers, categories, and navigation
- 🍽️ **Menu with Add-to-Cart** – beautifully structured, filterable food items
- 🛒 **Cart System** – add/remove items with smooth interactions
- 💸 **Real-Time Price Calculation with Discounts** – dynamic cart total updates based on discount offers
- 📅 **Reservation Page** – book your spot at the restaurant
- 🔐 **User Authentication** – sign up and log in securely
- 🎁 **Live Offers** – FOMO-driven offers for engagement
- 💬 **Feedback & Reviews** – allow users to leave ratings/comments
- ⚙️ **Responsive & Fast** – works great on all devices
